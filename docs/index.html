How it works

1. Pop-ups die on load
Cookie and consent banners, newsletter modals, "get the app" bars and scroll locks are hidden as the page loads. Nothing is clicked or accepted for you.
2. One key, calm page
Press Alt+Shift+R or click the icon. The article opens in your design. Esc brings the original back.
3. Pro: every tab, all session
Turn on session mode and every article you open is already calm. Or pick sites that always open calm.

No install? Use the bookmarklet

Drag this to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any article: Calmpage

Works in any desktop browser, Firefox included. A few sites block bookmarklets with strict security rules; the extension works there.

Install the extension

  1. Download calmpage-extension.zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions, brave://extensions) and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder. Done.

Questions

Doesn't my browser already have reader view?

It does, page by page, with its own look. It can't be switched on for a whole session, and on many sites the cookie banner sits on top of the page before you get that far. Calmpage clears the pop-ups first and uses your one design everywhere.

Does it accept cookies for me?

No. It hides the banner. It never clicks "accept".

Is it an ad blocker?

No. The free version leaves the original page alone apart from pop-ups and sticky bars. In reader view you only see the article, so ads aren't there. Pair it with uBlock Origin if you want both.

What does it send anywhere?

Nothing. No analytics, no server. Your settings sync through your browser's own storage.

How does Pro unlock?

After PayPal checkout you get your subscription ID. Paste it into Calmpage settings. It's checked on your device.
