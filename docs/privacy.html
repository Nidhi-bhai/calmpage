Privacy

The Calmpage extension and bookmarklet make no network requests and collect no data. Page content is processed only inside your browser. Settings (design, site lists, Pro key) are stored in your browser's own extension storage and may sync through your browser account.

Payments are handled by PayPal. This site receives only the subscription ID PayPal returns, which is shown back to you and not stored anywhere by us. This site uses no analytics or cookies of its own; the PayPal button loads PayPal's script.
